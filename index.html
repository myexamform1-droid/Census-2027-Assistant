Census 2027 — Occupation & Nature of Industry/Trade/Service Assistant

Describe the person's actual work in English or Hindi. The assistant separates what the person does (occupation) from the activity of the establishment/business (industry, trade or service). You can mix Hindi and English in the same description.

Important: The Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India (ORGI) says a separate Code Directory is being prepared for descriptive Census questions including Occupation and Nature of industry, trade or service. This prototype therefore gives a guideline-based classification and does not claim to reproduce an unpublished 2027 official code number.

How the decision system works

  1. First identify the person's actual work performed, not merely qualification or workplace.
  2. Then identify the principal activity of the establishment for Nature of industry, trade or service.
  3. Mention the establishment/business activity whenever possible.
  4. If the person performs more than one job, describe the main/current work relevant to the Census reference period.
  5. If the situation is ambiguous, the assistant asks for clarification instead of forcing a classification.

Reference basis: ORGI Census Division guidance states that descriptive/non-numeric entries will use a separate Code Directory, including Occupation and Nature of industry, trade or service. Historical Census 2011 occupational tables use occupational classification separately from industrial category.
